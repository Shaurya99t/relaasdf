Chapter seven · from me to you

A Letter
For You ♥

09.09.2026my jaan bacha

5 years… ❤️

Honestly, kabhi kabhi sochta hoon ki ye 5 saal itni jaldi kaise nikal gaye.

Jab peeche mudkar dekhta hoon toh realise hota hai ki humne saath mein kitna kuch experience kar liya — so many memories, hasi-mazaak, fights, misunderstandings, emotional moments, random conversations aur woh chhoti chhoti cheezein jo shayad kisi aur ke liye normal hongi, but mere liye hamesha special rahengi.

In these 5 years, hum dono bhi bohot change hue hain. Life change hui, situations change hui, humari priorities bhi change hui, but somehow ek cheez same rahi — humara ek dusre ke saath rehna.

Humara relationship kabhi perfect nahi raha, aur honestly, main chahta bhi nahi ki woh perfect ho. Hum lade hain, gussa hue hain, kabhi ek dusre ko samajhne mein fail hue hain, kabhi ek dusre ko hurt bhi kiya hai.

But every time, somehow, we found our way back to each other.

And I think that's what makes what we have so real.

Meri jaan bacha, tum kab meri life ka itna important part ban gayi, pata hi nahi chala.

Ab koi bhi achhi baat hoti hai toh sabse pehle tumhe batane ka mann karta hai, kuch bura ho toh tumse baat karne ka mann karta hai, aur kabhi kabhi bina kisi reason ke bhi bas tumse baat karne ka mann karta hai.

Tumhare saath ek alag sa comfort hai.

I can be completely myself with you — meri achhi side bhi, meri stupid side bhi, meri irritating habits bhi 😂 — aur phir bhi mujhe pata hota hai ki tum ho.

I think over these 5 years, my love for you has changed in the best possible way.

Pehle shayad love zyada excitement tha, but aaj uske saath understanding, comfort, respect aur ek strong feeling bhi hai ki haan, mujhe isi person ke saath apni life share karni hai.

Mujhe future ka exact plan nahi pata.

But itna zaroor pata hai ki jo bhi life humare saamne laaye, main tumhare saath face karna chahta hoon.

Mujhe tumhare saath sirf special days aur big moments nahi chahiye.

Mujhe woh normal life bhi chahiye — random plans, late-night talks, chhoti chhoti outings, stupid jokes, ek dusre ko unnecessarily irritate karna, kisi chhoti si baat pe ladna aur phir kuch der baad sab normal kar lena.

Main ye promise nahi karunga ki hum kabhi nahi ladenge ya life hamesha easy rahegi.

But I promise that whenever things get difficult, main humare relationship ko choose karunga.

Gusse mein bhi tumhe samajhne ki koshish karunga, ego ko humse bada nahi hone dunga, aur jab life difficult hogi toh tumhare saath khada rahunga.

I want us to always remember that it's never me against you — it's always us against the problem.

Thank you for these 5 years.

Thank you for staying, for understanding me, for tolerating my nonsense 😂, for being there through the good days and the not-so-good ones.

5 years later, I still look at you and feel lucky that it's you.

And if I had to choose all over again, knowing everything we've been through, I would still choose you.

Every single time.
♥

yours,
always

Chapter nine · the promise

To Our Next
Chapter ∞

a promise for year 6 and every year after

Aage kya hoga, honestly mujhe nahi pata.

Life mein kya changes aayenge, hum kahan honge, kitna busy honge, ye sab main predict nahi kar sakta.

But ek cheez ka promise hai, jaan bacha — main har situation mein tumhe apna person samajhunga.

Sirf achhe time mein nahi, balki jab sab kuch thoda messy ho, jab hum dono irritated ho, jab life difficult ho, tab bhi.

Mujhe tumhare saath woh everyday wali life jeeni hai.

Random conversations.
Chhoti chhoti outings.
Stupid jokes.
Late-night talks.
Ek dusre ko pareshan karna.
Aur years later bhi ek dusre ki same stupid habits pe hasna.

Main perfect future promise nahi kar raha.

Bas itna promise hai ki main tumhe choose karta rahunga.

“Haan... humne saath mein bohot accha kiya.”
